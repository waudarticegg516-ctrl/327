About BRCLpro

Founded in Exeter, England, BRCLpro is a leading financial information consulting firm committed to delivering value-added financial services.

Trusted Financial Partner

BRCLpro is headquartered at 82 King William St, Exeter, England. We focus on financial information analysis, market research, investment consulting and risk assessment.

With years of industry experience, our team serves enterprises, institutions and individual investors across multiple regions. We adhere to professionalism, integrity and efficiency to create stable returns and reliable strategies for every client.

We keep pace with global financial trends, continuously optimize service systems, and maintain long-term stable cooperative relationships with partners.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to empower clients with accurate financial information and strategic insights, enabling them to make informed decisions in an increasingly complex global market.

We aim to become the most trusted financial consulting partner in Europe, recognized for our deep industry knowledge, personalized service, and commitment to client success.

We are committed to sustainable development, integrating ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) factors into our consulting services to help clients achieve both financial returns and social value.

Core Values

Professionalism: Our team consists of certified financial analysts, CFA charterholders, and industry experts with extensive experience in global financial markets.

Integrity: We maintain the highest ethical standards in all our operations, ensuring transparency and accountability in every client engagement.

Innovation: We continuously invest in technology and research to develop cutting-edge analytical tools and methodologies for financial forecasting and risk management.

Client-Centric: Every solution is tailored to meet the unique needs and goals of our clients, with a focus on long-term value creation rather than short-term gains.

Our Professional Team

Our core team consists of senior financial experts, analysts and consultants with rich practical experience.

Michael Brown

Chief Financial Analyst

Michael has over 15 years of experience in financial market analysis, specializing in European and North American markets. He holds a CFA Level 3 certification and a Master's degree in Financial Economics from the University of Oxford.

Before joining BRCLpro, he worked as a senior analyst at a top international investment bank, providing strategic advice to institutional clients. His expertise includes quantitative analysis, market trend forecasting, and portfolio optimization.

Michael leads our market analysis team, ensuring that our clients receive the most accurate and timely financial insights to support their investment decisions.

Sarah Wilson

Senior Consultant

Sarah is a seasoned financial consultant with a focus on corporate financial strategy and risk management. She has worked with over 50 medium and large enterprises across various industries, including manufacturing, technology, and healthcare.

She holds an MBA from London Business School and is a certified Financial Risk Manager (FRM). Her approach combines strategic thinking with practical implementation, helping clients optimize their financial operations and mitigate risks.

At BRCLpro, Sarah oversees the enterprise consulting division, leading a team of specialists to deliver customized financial solutions for corporate clients.

David Clark

Risk Control Director

David is an expert in financial risk management with over 20 years of experience in the banking and financial services industry. He previously served as the risk control director at a major UK bank, responsible for developing and implementing risk management frameworks.

He is a Fellow of the Institute of Risk Management (IRM) and has extensive knowledge in credit risk, market risk, and operational risk assessment. His work at BRCLpro focuses on helping clients build robust risk management systems and comply with regulatory requirements.

David also leads our financial training programs, sharing his expertise with clients and internal teams to enhance risk awareness and management capabilities.
